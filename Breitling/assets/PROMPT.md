# Dial artwork provenance

Mode: image edit using the built-in image-generation tool, with the user's watch photograph as the reference. `dial.png` preserves the original generated image. Firmware resizes it to 240 x 240 and packs RGB565 with `prepare_assets.py`; hands and LCD text are drawn by C++ at runtime.

## Bezel correction

The current `dial.png` was edited with the built-in image-generation tool to place exactly four gold bezel rider tabs at 12, 3, 6 and 9. The previous source is preserved as `dial-before-marker-fix.png`.

### Correction prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied handless watch dial texture. Correct ONLY the OUTER metal bezel: remove every existing gold rider tab at diagonal or intermediate positions, replacing those areas with matching brushed silver/titanium bezel and engraved markings. Add exactly FOUR gold rider tabs centered precisely at the cardinal clock positions: 12 o'clock (top center, engraved 0), 3 o'clock (right center, engraved 15), 6 o'clock (bottom center, engraved 30), and 9 o'clock (left center, engraved 45). There must be NO gold bezel tabs anywhere else. Ensure the bezel minute numbering runs correctly clockwise from 0 at top, through 5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45,50,55. Preserve the entire INNER blue dial exactly: same gold 12/3/6/9 numerals, same tick marks, same lettering and logos, identical two blank black LCD windows at exactly the same positions and sizes. No hands, no center pin, no LCD text. Preserve circular framing, center, dimensions, black corners, lighting and realistic worn metal texture. The output must be one single full watch dial, not a grid or collage.
